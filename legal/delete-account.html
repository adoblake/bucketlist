Delete your Bucketlist account

You can delete your account and its data at any time. You don't need to reinstall anything.

Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Bucketlist and go to Groups & chat. Sign in the same way you usually do.
  2. Tap Account at the top of the page.
  3. Tap Delete my account…, type DELETE, and confirm.

Open Account settings

Can't sign in?

Contact __SUPPORT_CONTACT__ and include:

  • Your display name
  • How you signed in (ChatGPT, Google through ChatGPT, or email)
  • For email sign-in, your email address

We may ask you to confirm the request from that account or email. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your display name and account record, and your rules acceptance
  • Every message you sent, and your group memberships
  • Your game sign-ups, court confirmations, and correction notes
  • Court photos you uploaded
  • Your block list, and anyone's block of you
  • Alert subscriptions on all your devices

What happens to shared things

  • Groups you created: they pass to the longest-standing member. If you're the only member, the group is deleted.
  • Games you host: upcoming games are cancelled and players are notified. Past games stay in other players' history as hosted by "Former player".
  • Court pins you added: they stay on the map so other players' games still work. They're shown as "Added by a player", without your name.
  • Reports you sent: they stay so moderators can deal with abuse, but your identity is removed from them.

If you signed in with email, your address may stay with our sign-in provider for up to 30 days, after which we remove it. Removing it doesn't affect your ChatGPT or Google account.
