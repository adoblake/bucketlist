Bucketlist is for finding courts and getting real games going. These rules keep it safe and useful for everyone. Using Bucketlist means you agree to them.

Who can use Bucketlist

You must be 18 or older. Use one account, and pick a display name that doesn't pretend to be someone else.

Not allowed, anywhere

Bucketlist has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. Don't post, send, or upload:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging violence or self-harm
  • Hate speech or attacks on people for who they are
  • Sexual content or nudity, or any content that sexualises minors
  • Other people's private information, such as phone numbers, addresses, or photos of people without permission
  • Spam, scams, ads, or links meant to mislead
  • Fake courts, deliberately wrong pins, or games you don't intend to hold
  • Anything illegal, including selling drugs or weapons

Reporting and enforcement

You can report any message, player, court, court photo, or game from inside the app. You can also block a player so you no longer see their messages. Moderators review reports. They may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break these rules, without warning in serious cases. Suspended players can still read their groups and delete their account.

Your content

You own what you post. You give Bucketlist permission to store and display it in the app so it works as intended. For example, we show your court pin on the map and your messages to your group. This permission ends when you delete the content, except for anything other players have already relied on. For example, a court pin you added stays on the map without your name.

Playing safely

  • Court locations, access, hours, and conditions come from public records and other players. They are not verified. A game in Bucketlist doesn't reserve a court.
  • Meet in public, play within your limits, and use your own judgement about who you meet. Bucketlist doesn't screen players. It isn't responsible for what happens at a game.
  • If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first. Then report them in the app.

The service

Bucketlist is provided as is. Features may change, and the service may sometimes be unavailable. You can stop using Bucketlist and delete your account at any time. We may update these rules. When we make important changes, we'll ask you to accept them again in the app.
