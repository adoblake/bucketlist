Privacy policy

Effective __EFFECTIVE_DATE__

Bucketlist helps people find basketball courts and organize pickup games in Arizona. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

When you sign in

  • Account identifier. A stable ID from the sign-in method you choose: ChatGPT, Google through ChatGPT, or an email code. We use it to recognise your account. It is never shown to other players.
  • Email address. If you use an email code, your email goes to our sign-in provider (Supabase) to send and check the code. We don't show it to other players. For abuse limits, we keep a one-way hash of it for up to two hours.
  • Display name. The name or nickname you choose.
  • Rules acceptance. The date you accepted the community rules, and which version.

When you use shared features

  • Groups and messages: the groups you create or join, their names and descriptions, and the messages you send.
  • Courts: court pins you add, with the park name, city, notes, and photo. Court confirmations and corrections you send.
  • Games: games you host (title, time, notes, player limit) and the games you join.
  • Safety records: reports you send, and players you block.
  • Alerts: if you turn on alerts, the push address your browser gives us for this device.
  • Abuse limits: a one-way hash of your IP address during email sign-in, kept for up to two hours.

Location

If you allow location, your device uses it to centre the map. We don't send your precise location to our servers or store it. To show courts, the app sends the edges of the map area on screen, not your position, to our servers and to the map providers listed below.

Before uploading a court photo, the app re-saves it on your device. This removes hidden metadata such as GPS coordinates.

Stored only on your device

Your appearance setting, the sample game planner, sample stats, and the demo profile stay in your browser's storage. They are never uploaded.

Who can see what

  • Anyone: court pins, park names, court notes, court photos, and shared game details, including meeting notes and player counts. Player-added courts are labelled "Added by a player". They don't show your name.
  • People in your groups: your display name and the messages you send there.
  • People in the same game: your display name, once they have joined too.
  • Bucketlist moderators: reported content and the reason given. They can see who sent a report. The reported person can't.

We don't sell your information. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with data brokers.

Services we rely on

  • Hosting and database: store the data described above.
  • OpenAI: handles sign-in if you choose Continue with ChatGPT or Google through ChatGPT.
  • Supabase: handles email-code sign-in.
  • OpenStreetMap: provides map images. Its place search (Nominatim) receives the place names you search for. Its court lookup (Overpass) receives the visible map area.
  • Esri World Imagery: receives a court's coordinates to show its aerial photo.
  • Google Maps: receives a court's location, but only when you tap Directions.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple, or Mozilla): delivers alerts. Alert text never includes message content.

These services receive your device's IP address when your device contacts them. Their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content until you delete it or delete your account. Abuse-limit records expire within two hours. We keep reports so we can deal with repeated abuse. When you delete your account, reports you sent stay, but your identity is removed from them.

Deleting your data

Delete any message, court, or group you created from inside the app. To delete your whole account, open Groups & chat → Account → Delete my account. See how account deletion works.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in sessions are kept in secure, HttpOnly cookies. Our servers check permissions on every request. For example, only members can read a group's messages.

Age

Bucketlist is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
